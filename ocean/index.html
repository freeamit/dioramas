About

Ocean Diorama v1.5. Water, sky and island rendering: Ultra Water (MIT licence, © 2026 Humpalumps), adapted by Amitav.

Seaplane model: “Gottfried Freiherr von Banfield's seaplane” by BadPizza (https://sketchfab.com/BadPizza), licensed CC-BY-4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Compressed and recoloured crimson/white.

Harbour boat: “Boat” by Pikas (https://sketchfab.com/Pikas), CC-BY-4.0. Beach houses: “Holiday Beach House” by Chua Shan Lim (https://sketchfab.com/kosukeyuuma), CC-BY-4.0. Fishermen's shacks: “Sea Shack” by Kimppo (https://sketchfab.com/Kimppo), CC-BY-4.0. All three compressed (meshopt/WebP), simplified and rescaled; licence https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. Trees, palms, flowers, rocks, houses, lighthouse, dock, sea traffic (sailboats, fishing boats, tug, ferry), buoys, gulls, dolphins, stars, crescent moon and display glass are procedural.
