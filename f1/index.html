F1 Circuit Diorama v1.4

A three.js diorama of seven circuits (Shanghai, Kleine Nordschleife, Bugatti Le Mans, an oval, South Garda, Mini Catalunya and a club track) with 1–24 F1 cars (one hero car by default), an automatic day/night cycle, floodlights, start gantry, live leaderboard and an optional player car.

Views bar: Overview, Top, Pit lane, Chase, Onboard, TV drift, Tour and corner presets; cameras glide with exponential easing (camera style inspired by whistlevale.com). Drag to orbit, pinch/wheel to zoom, two-finger or right-drag to pan, double-tap to focus the car. Tap a driver in the leaderboard to follow that car. Gamepad: left stick steer, RT gas, LT/B brake, A next view, LB/RB previous/next view, D-pad Overview/Tour/Chase/Onboard, right stick orbit, Y time of day, X follow next car.
